Comprehensive statistical breakdown of pricing medians, tails, and operational realities across major tourist hubs.

San Jose & Liberia (Transit Hubs)

High volume, 1-2 night stays, minimal seasonality.

Analysis: Extremely tight bell curve. Demand is highly predictable as it is tied to airport arrivals/departures. Low utility costs because ceiling fans generally suffice year-round. Profit is volume-driven, not margin-driven.

Tamarindo & Jaco (Party/Surf Coastal)

Longer stays, high bar revenue, massive seasonality.

MetricLow (10%)MedianHigh (90%)
Dorm Bed$18$25$40
Private Rm$60$90$150
Monthly Utils$1,500$2,500$4,000

Analysis: Wide statistical distribution. Budget spots exist alongside luxury "poshtels" (e.g., Selina). High variance in expenses due to mandatory 24/7 Air Conditioning. Massive F&B upside due to nightlife/surf culture.

Arenal & Monteverde (Eco/Adventure)

Tour commission dependent, temperate climate.

MetricLow (10%)MedianHigh (90%)
Dorm Bed$15$20$30
Private Rm$45$70$110
Monthly Utils$500$800$1,500
F&B Upsell$10$20$40
Analysis: Cooler elevations eliminate AC costs, lowering overhead. Bed rates are average, but total lifetime value of the guest is massive because hostels act as booking agents for ziplining/rafting, taking 15-25% commissions.

Osa Peninsula (Remote Deep Jungle)

Captive audience, extreme logistics costs.

MetricLow (10%)MedianHigh (90%)
Dorm Bed$20$35$50
Private Rm$80$120$250
Monthly Utils$800$1,500$3,000
F&B Upsell$25$50$90
Analysis: Operations are brutally expensive (transporting food, maintaining off-grid solar). However, guests are a "captive audience." With no other restaurants around, 100% of meals are bought in-house, drastically raising per-guest F&B spend.
